# Async document-processing pipeline

**Client:** Hale & Rowe LLP (synthetic) | **Industry:** Legal | **Duration:** 3 weeks | **Date:** 2025-03

## The problem
The client was a law firm whose upload page timed out on 300-page bundles.

## What I did
My approach: moved OCR and classification into background workers with retries and progress polling. The main stack was Python, FastAPI. Under the hood this meant asynchronous workers pulling tasks off a broker. It also needed an in-memory cache in front of the database. Behind the scenes I set up a containerised build that runs the same everywhere. 

## Result
The work uploads of any size returned in under a second; 0 lost documents in 3 months.

## What I learned
Ship a thin end-to-end slice first, then improve the slowest part.
