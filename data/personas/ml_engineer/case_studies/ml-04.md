# Retail demand forecasting for 300 SKUs

**Client:** FreshMart (synthetic) | **Industry:** Retail | **Duration:** 6 weeks | **Date:** 2023-09

## The problem
The client was a grocery chain over-ordering perishables every holiday.

## What I did
My approach: per-SKU forecasters with holiday regressors, retrained weekly. The main stack was Python, Time series forecasting, Prophet. Under the hood this meant dataframe wrangling of messy exports. It also needed scheduled DAGs that orchestrated the nightly loads. 

## Result
The work reduced MAPE from 31% to 18% and cut waste on perishables by 12%.

## What I learned
Ship a thin end-to-end slice first, then improve the slowest part.
