# Support-ticket triage classifier

**Client:** Helpwise (synthetic) | **Industry:** SaaS | **Duration:** 4 weeks | **Date:** 2023-03

## The problem
The client was a support team drowning in 2,000 untagged tickets a week.

## What I did
My approach: TF-IDF features with a calibrated linear model, served behind a small HTTP service. The main stack was Python, scikit-learn, NLP, Model evaluation. Under the hood this meant dataframe wrangling of messy exports. It also needed a typed async Python API with auto-generated docs. 

## Result
The work cut manual tagging time by 70% and reached 0.91 macro-F1 on a hold-out month.

## What I learned
Keep the client's operators in the loop weekly; their feedback changed the design twice.
