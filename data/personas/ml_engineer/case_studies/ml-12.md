# Sentiment dashboard for app reviews

**Client:** PixelForge Studio (synthetic) | **Industry:** Mobile apps | **Duration:** 3 weeks | **Date:** 2023-01

## The problem
The client was a mobile studio unable to read 40,000 app-store reviews.

## What I did
My approach: a pretrained sentiment model plus topic clustering, refreshed nightly. The main stack was NLP, Hugging Face Transformers. Under the hood this meant dataframe wrangling of messy exports. 

## Result
The work surfaced 3 crash-related themes that drove the next two releases.

## What I learned
Ship a thin end-to-end slice first, then improve the slowest part.
