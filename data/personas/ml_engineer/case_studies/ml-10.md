# Semantic product search

**Client:** Kicks & Co (synthetic) | **Industry:** E-commerce | **Duration:** 3 weeks | **Date:** 2024-10

## The problem
The client was shoppers getting zero results for 'shoes for a beach wedding'.

## What I did
My approach: embedding search over product text with a keyword fallback. The main stack was RAG, Python. Under the hood this meant a hosted similarity-search service. It also needed a typed async Python API with auto-generated docs. We looked at FAISS early on but decided not to use it for this engagement.

## Result
The work zero-result searches fell from 14% to 3%.

## What I learned
Agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; it saved two rounds of rework.
