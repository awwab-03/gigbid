# Defect detection on a bottling line

**Client:** AquaPure Bottlers (synthetic) | **Industry:** Manufacturing | **Duration:** 7 weeks | **Date:** 2024-05

## The problem
The client was a bottling plant catching cracked caps only in manual spot checks.

## What I did
My approach: a fine-tuned object detector exported for CPU inference on the line PC. The main stack was Computer vision, PyTorch, YOLO. Under the hood this meant frame-by-frame image preprocessing. It also needed exporting the model to a portable runtime for CPU inference. We looked at TensorFlow early on but decided not to use it for this engagement.

## Result
The work 98.2% recall on cracked caps at 25 frames per second on a CPU.

## What I learned
Ship a thin end-to-end slice first, then improve the slowest part.
