# Invoice field extraction

**Client:** LedgerPoint (synthetic) | **Industry:** Finance | **Duration:** 5 weeks | **Date:** 2024-08

## The problem
The client was an accounting firm keying invoice totals by hand.

## What I did
My approach: OCR plus entity extraction with validation rules for totals and dates. The main stack was NLP, spaCy, Python. Under the hood this meant frame-by-frame image preprocessing. It also needed a typed async Python API with auto-generated docs. 

## Result
The work 94% field accuracy, saving roughly 30 staff hours a week.

## What I learned
Write the data checks before the model or the UI; most surprises came from the data.
