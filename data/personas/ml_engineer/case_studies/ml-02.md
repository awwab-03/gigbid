# Customer churn early-warning model

**Client:** NorthLine Telecom (synthetic) | **Industry:** Telecom | **Duration:** 6 weeks | **Date:** 2023-06

## The problem
The client was a regional telecom losing subscribers without warning.

## What I did
My approach: gradient-boosted trees on usage and billing history, with SHAP explanations for account managers. The main stack was Python, XGBoost, Churn prediction, Model evaluation. Under the hood this meant dataframe wrangling of messy exports. It also needed experiment tracking with a model registry. We looked at Spark early on but decided not to use it for this engagement.

## Result
The work flagged 64% of churners in the top decile, retention campaign saved an estimated 1,100 accounts.

## What I learned
Agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; it saved two rounds of rework.
