# Experiment tracking rollout

**Client:** Nimbus Credit (synthetic) | **Industry:** Fintech | **Duration:** 3 weeks | **Date:** 2024-03

## The problem
The client was a fintech data team that could not reproduce last quarter's credit model.

## What I did
My approach: central experiment tracking, a model registry and promotion gates in the pipeline. The main stack was MLflow, Python. Under the hood this meant a containerised build that runs the same everywhere. It also needed every push tested and deployed automatically. 

## Result
The work every production model traceable to code, data and metrics; audit passed first time.

## What I learned
Write the data checks before the model or the UI; most surprises came from the data.
