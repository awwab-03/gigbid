# Fine-tuned tone model for customer emails

**Client:** Brightdesk (synthetic) | **Industry:** SaaS | **Duration:** 4 weeks | **Date:** 2025-04

## The problem
The client was a SaaS company whose AI replies sounded nothing like their brand.

## What I did
My approach: LoRA adapters on a 7B open model trained on 4,000 approved replies. The main stack was LLM fine-tuning, Hugging Face Transformers, PyTorch. Under the hood this meant scripting the pipeline end to end. We looked at OpenAI API early on but decided not to use it for this engagement.

## Result
The work brand-voice rating from reviewers rose from 2.9 to 4.3 out of 5.

## What I learned
Ship a thin end-to-end slice first, then improve the slowest part.
