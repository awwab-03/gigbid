# Product recommendation engine

**Client:** StyleLane (synthetic) | **Industry:** E-commerce | **Duration:** 5 weeks | **Date:** 2023-11

## The problem
The client was an online fashion store with a generic 'popular items' carousel.

## What I did
My approach: implicit-feedback matrix factorisation blended with recent-view similarity. The main stack was Python, Recommendation systems. Under the hood this meant dataframe wrangling of messy exports. It also needed vectorised array maths. Behind the scenes I set up an in-memory cache in front of the database. 

## Result
The work click-through on recommendations rose 23% in a two-week A/B test.

## What I learned
Keep the client's operators in the loop weekly; their feedback changed the design twice.
