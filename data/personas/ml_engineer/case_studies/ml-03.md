# Internal policy assistant with citations

**Client:** Crescent Insurance (synthetic) | **Industry:** Insurance | **Duration:** 5 weeks | **Date:** 2024-02

## The problem
The client was claims staff searching 900 pages of policy PDFs by hand.

## What I did
My approach: hybrid retrieval over chunked policies with page-level citations and a refusal when nothing matched. The main stack was Python, RAG, LangChain, OpenAI API. Under the hood this meant an in-memory nearest-neighbour index over embeddings. It also needed a typed async Python API with auto-generated docs. Behind the scenes I set up a containerised build that runs the same everywhere. We looked at Pinecone early on but decided not to use it for this engagement.

## Result
The work answered 82% of a 120-question test set correctly with citations, average answer time 4 seconds.

## What I learned
Keep the client's operators in the loop weekly; their feedback changed the design twice.
