# Lead scoring for a B2B sales team

**Client:** Pipeline Pro (synthetic) | **Industry:** SaaS | **Duration:** 4 weeks | **Date:** 2025-06

## The problem
The client was sales reps calling leads in random order.

## What I did
My approach: boosted trees on CRM activity with monotonic constraints the sales lead could reason about. The main stack was LightGBM, Model evaluation, Python. Under the hood this meant hand-written queries with window functions. It also needed dataframe wrangling of messy exports. 

## Result
The work top-scored 20% of leads converted at 3.1x the base rate.

## What I learned
Write the data checks before the model or the UI; most surprises came from the data.
