# Survey analysis in R

**Client:** Pakistan Youth Survey (synthetic) | **Industry:** Research | **Duration:** 4 weeks | **Date:** 2022-08

## The problem
The client was an NGO with 6,000 survey responses and no analyst.

## What I did
My approach: weighted estimates, regression on outcomes and a clear PDF report. The main stack was R, Statistics, Data visualization. Under the hood this meant deduplicating and standardising messy records. 

## Result
The work report cited in the NGO's grant renewal that secured funding for two more years.

## What I learned
Ship a thin end-to-end slice first, then improve the slowest part.
