# Revenue forecasting model

**Client:** Ledgerly (synthetic) | **Industry:** SaaS | **Duration:** 3 weeks | **Date:** 2025-01

## The problem
The client was a SaaS finance team forecasting revenue with gut feel.

## What I did
My approach: a driver-based model of new, expansion and churned revenue with scenario inputs. The main stack was Forecasting, Python, Excel. Under the hood this meant confidence intervals and significance tests. It also needed dataframe wrangling of messy exports. We looked at Prophet early on but decided not to use it for this engagement.

## Result
The work quarterly forecast error fell from 14% to 4%.

## What I learned
Agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; it saved two rounds of rework.
