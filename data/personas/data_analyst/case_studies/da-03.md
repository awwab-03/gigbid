# dbt project for a fintech warehouse

**Client:** Coinvault (synthetic) | **Industry:** Fintech | **Duration:** 6 weeks | **Date:** 2024-02

## The problem
The client was a fintech with 200 untested SQL views nobody trusted.

## What I did
My approach: layered staging, intermediate and mart models with tests and docs. The main stack was dbt, Snowflake, SQL. Under the hood this meant scheduled DAGs that orchestrated the nightly loads. It also needed managed connectors syncing SaaS sources into the warehouse. 

## Result
The work data incidents fell from weekly to one per quarter.

## What I learned
Keep the client's operators in the loop weekly; their feedback changed the design twice.
