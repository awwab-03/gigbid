# Executive sales dashboard

**Client:** UrbanMart (synthetic) | **Industry:** Retail | **Duration:** 3 weeks | **Date:** 2023-02

## The problem
The client was a retail chain whose leadership got a 40-tab spreadsheet every Monday.

## What I did
My approach: a star schema and one-page KPI report with drill-through by region. The main stack was Power BI, SQL, Dashboarding. Under the hood this meant spreadsheet models with pivot tables and lookups. We looked at Tableau early on but decided not to use it for this engagement.

## Result
The work weekly reporting time fell from 6 hours to 20 minutes.

## What I learned
Agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; it saved two rounds of rework.
