# Marketing attribution warehouse

**Client:** Wildthread Apparel (synthetic) | **Industry:** E-commerce | **Duration:** 5 weeks | **Date:** 2023-07

## The problem
The client was a D2C brand unable to tell which channel drove sales.

## What I did
My approach: joined ad spend with web events and orders, then built tested models for each channel. The main stack was BigQuery, SQL, GA4. Under the hood this meant a modelling layer of tested SQL views. It also needed free Google dashboards connected to Sheets. 

## Result
The work reallocated 18% of ad budget and lowered cost per order by 22%.

## What I learned
Ship a thin end-to-end slice first, then improve the slowest part.
