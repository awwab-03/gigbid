# Redshift cost and performance tuning

**Client:** StreamWave Media (synthetic) | **Industry:** Media | **Duration:** 3 weeks | **Date:** 2024-11

## The problem
The client was a media company whose dashboards took two minutes to load.

## What I did
My approach: sort and distribution keys, materialised aggregates and query rewrites. The main stack was Redshift, SQL. Under the hood this meant hosting on Amazon's cloud with object storage and managed databases. We looked at BigQuery early on but decided not to use it for this engagement.

## Result
The work median dashboard query from 110 s to 9 s and 30% lower cluster spend.

## What I learned
Ship a thin end-to-end slice first, then improve the slowest part.
