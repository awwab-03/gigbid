# E-commerce cohort and LTV dashboard

**Client:** Kettle & Co (synthetic) | **Industry:** E-commerce | **Duration:** 2 weeks | **Date:** 2025-04

## The problem
The client was a Shopify brand guessing customer lifetime value.

## What I did
My approach: monthly cohorts, repeat-purchase curves and LTV by first product. The main stack was Looker Studio, BigQuery, SQL. Under the hood this meant web analytics events and conversion funnels. It also needed a single page of KPIs the managers check every morning. 

## Result
The work shifted acquisition to two hero products with 1.8x higher LTV.

## What I learned
Keep the client's operators in the loop weekly; their feedback changed the design twice.
