# A/B test analysis for pricing page

**Client:** Formstack Lite (synthetic) | **Industry:** SaaS | **Duration:** 2 weeks | **Date:** 2024-05

## The problem
The client was a SaaS team arguing over a pricing-page test with no clear result.

## What I did
My approach: pre-registered metrics, sample-size checks and a sequential test. The main stack was A/B testing, Statistics, Python. Under the hood this meant hand-written queries with window functions. 

## Result
The work declared a winner with 95% confidence; annual-plan uptake rose 11%.

## What I learned
Agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; it saved two rounds of rework.
