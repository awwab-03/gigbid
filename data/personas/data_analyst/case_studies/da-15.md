# Fivetran and Snowflake setup

**Client:** Orbitly (synthetic) | **Industry:** SaaS | **Duration:** 2 weeks | **Date:** 2025-06

## The problem
The client was a startup with data scattered across HubSpot, Stripe and Postgres.

## What I did
My approach: managed connectors into a raw layer, then tested models for finance. The main stack was Snowflake, Fivetran. Under the hood this meant a modelling layer of tested SQL views. It also needed hand-written queries with window functions. 

## Result

## What I learned
