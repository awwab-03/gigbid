# Data cleaning of a 2-million-row CRM export

**Client:** Prime Distributors (synthetic) | **Industry:** B2B | **Duration:** 2 weeks | **Date:** 2023-12

## The problem
The client was a B2B distributor with duplicated, mis-typed customer records.

## What I did
My approach: fuzzy matching, address standardisation and a review sheet for edge cases. The main stack was Data cleaning, Python, pandas. Under the hood this meant spreadsheet models with pivot tables and lookups. 

## Result
The work 2.1 million rows reduced to 640,000 unique customers with an audit trail.

## What I learned
Keep the client's operators in the loop weekly; their feedback changed the design twice.
