# Nightly ETL from five SaaS tools

**Client:** Brightpath Agency (synthetic) | **Industry:** Agency | **Duration:** 4 weeks | **Date:** 2024-08

## The problem
The client was an agency copying CSVs from five tools every morning.

## What I did
My approach: scheduled loaders with retries, deduplication and a reconciliation report. The main stack was ETL, Python, PostgreSQL. Under the hood this meant scheduled DAGs that orchestrated the nightly loads. It also needed deduplicating and standardising messy records. We looked at Snowflake early on but decided not to use it for this engagement.

## Result
The work saved 10 staff hours a week; zero missed loads in 4 months.

## What I learned
Keep the client's operators in the loop weekly; their feedback changed the design twice.
