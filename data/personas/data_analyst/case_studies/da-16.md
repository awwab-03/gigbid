# Inventory KPI dashboard in Excel

**Client:** Indus Textiles (synthetic) | **Industry:** Manufacturing | **Duration:** 1 weeks | **Date:** 2022-02

## The problem
The client was a small factory with no view of slow-moving stock.

## What I did
My approach: pivot models, lookups and a printable KPI sheet. The main stack was Excel, Dashboarding. Under the hood this meant clear charts that tell one story each. We looked at Power BI early on but decided not to use it for this engagement.

## Result
The work freed PKR 4 million of tied-up inventory within a quarter.

## What I learned
Keep the client's operators in the loop weekly; their feedback changed the design twice.
