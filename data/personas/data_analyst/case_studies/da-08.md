# Google Sheets automation for a non-profit

**Client:** HopeBridge Foundation (synthetic) | **Industry:** Non-profit | **Duration:** 2 weeks | **Date:** 2022-05

## The problem
The client was a non-profit merging donor lists by hand.

## What I did
My approach: scripted imports, duplicate detection and a free donor dashboard. The main stack was Google Sheets, Looker Studio. Under the hood this meant deduplicating and standardising messy records. 

## Result
The work duplicate donor records cut by 92%.

## What I learned
Ship a thin end-to-end slice first, then improve the slowest part.
