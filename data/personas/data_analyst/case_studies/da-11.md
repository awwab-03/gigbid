# Metabase self-serve analytics

**Client:** FleetFlow (synthetic) | **Industry:** Logistics | **Duration:** 3 weeks | **Date:** 2023-04

## The problem
The client was ops managers waiting days for every data question.

## What I did
My approach: curated models and 25 saved questions with plain-language descriptions. The main stack was Metabase, PostgreSQL, SQL. Under the hood this meant a containerised build that runs the same everywhere. 

## Result
The work ad-hoc data requests to the engineering team down 65%.

## What I learned
Agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; it saved two rounds of rework.
