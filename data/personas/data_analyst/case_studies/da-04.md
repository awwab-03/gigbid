# Churn analysis for a subscription box

**Client:** BoxChef (synthetic) | **Industry:** Subscription | **Duration:** 3 weeks | **Date:** 2023-10

## The problem
The client was a meal-kit company losing 9% of subscribers monthly.

## What I did
My approach: cohort retention curves and survival analysis by acquisition channel. The main stack was Python, pandas, Statistics. Under the hood this meant hand-written queries with window functions. It also needed clear charts that tell one story each. We looked at XGBoost early on but decided not to use it for this engagement.

## Result
The work identified a first-box issue; fixing it cut month-1 churn by 3 points.

## What I learned
Ship a thin end-to-end slice first, then improve the slowest part.
