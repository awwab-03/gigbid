# Tableau operations workbook

**Client:** CityCare Hospital (synthetic) | **Industry:** Healthcare | **Duration:** 4 weeks | **Date:** 2022-11

## The problem
The client was a hospital tracking bed occupancy on whiteboards.

## What I did
My approach: a workbook fed from the admissions database with hourly refresh. The main stack was Tableau, SQL. Under the hood this meant deduplicating and standardising messy records. It also needed spreadsheet models with pivot tables and lookups. We looked at Power BI early on but decided not to use it for this engagement.

## Result
The work average discharge delay reduced by 50 minutes.

## What I learned
Keep the client's operators in the loop weekly; their feedback changed the design twice.
