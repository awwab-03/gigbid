# Containerised deployment pipeline

**Client:** Launchpad Labs (synthetic) | **Industry:** SaaS | **Duration:** 3 weeks | **Date:** 2024-09

## The problem
The client was a startup deploying by SSH-ing into one server.

## What I did
My approach: container images, automated tests and blue-green deploys. The main stack was Docker, CI/CD, AWS. Under the hood this meant container orchestration with autoscaling pods. 

## Result
The work deploys went from 40 minutes and manual to 6 minutes and automatic.

## What I learned
Agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; it saved two rounds of rework.
