# WordPress membership site

**Client:** SpiceTrail Kitchen (synthetic) | **Industry:** Media | **Duration:** 4 weeks | **Date:** 2022-03

## The problem
The client was a cooking blog wanting paid recipes.

## What I did
My approach: a custom plugin for members-only content and recurring payments. The main stack was WordPress, JavaScript. Under the hood this meant subscription billing and checkout. 

## Result
The work 900 paying members in the first six months.

## What I learned
Write the data checks before the model or the UI; most surprises came from the data.
