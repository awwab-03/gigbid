# Shopify theme and custom checkout upsell

**Client:** GlowRoot Skincare (synthetic) | **Industry:** E-commerce | **Duration:** 3 weeks | **Date:** 2023-12

## The problem
The client was a skincare brand with a slow, generic theme.

## What I did
My approach: a custom theme with lazy media and a post-purchase upsell block. The main stack was Shopify, JavaScript. Under the hood this meant utility-first styling. We looked at WordPress early on but decided not to use it for this engagement.

## Result
The work mobile conversion rose from 1.4% to 2.2%.

## What I learned
Keep the client's operators in the loop weekly; their feedback changed the design twice.
