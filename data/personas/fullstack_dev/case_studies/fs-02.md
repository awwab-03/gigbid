# Marketplace for local tutors

**Client:** TutorNest (synthetic) | **Industry:** Education | **Duration:** 8 weeks | **Date:** 2023-08

## The problem
The client was a tutoring agency matching students by phone calls.

## What I did
My approach: server-rendered search pages, booking calendar and payouts. The main stack was Next.js, TypeScript, Prisma, PostgreSQL. Under the hood this meant sign in with Google and GitHub. It also needed subscription billing and checkout. We looked at GraphQL early on but decided not to use it for this engagement.

## Result
The work 1,800 bookings in the first two months, page load under 1.2 s.

## What I learned
Agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; it saved two rounds of rework.
