# Async report generation for a SaaS admin

**Client:** Insightly HR (synthetic) | **Industry:** SaaS | **Duration:** 2 weeks | **Date:** 2024-11

## The problem
The client was CSV exports timing out for large customers.

## What I did
My approach: queued export jobs with email links when ready. The main stack was Django, Python. Under the hood this meant a background task queue that retried failed jobs. It also needed an in-memory cache in front of the database. 

## Result
The work exports of 2 million rows complete without timeouts.

## What I learned
Write the data checks before the model or the UI; most surprises came from the data.
