# MongoDB event store for an IoT dashboard

**Client:** SunGrid Solar (synthetic) | **Industry:** Energy | **Duration:** 5 weeks | **Date:** 2024-03

## The problem
The client was a solar installer with sensor data in CSV dumps.

## What I did
My approach: time-bucketed documents and a live dashboard per site. The main stack was MongoDB, Node.js, React. Under the hood this meant live updates pushed to the browser without refresh. 

## Result
The work faults spotted 2 days sooner on average.

## What I learned
Ship a thin end-to-end slice first, then improve the slowest part.
