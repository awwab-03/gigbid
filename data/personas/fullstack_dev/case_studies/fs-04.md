# Django CRM for a property agency

**Client:** Skyline Estates (synthetic) | **Industry:** Real estate | **Duration:** 7 weeks | **Date:** 2022-10

## The problem
The client was agents tracking leads in WhatsApp groups.

## What I did
My approach: a Django app with an admin panel, lead pipeline and scheduled email reminders. The main stack was Django, PostgreSQL, REST API. Under the hood this meant a background task queue that retried failed jobs. It also needed a containerised build that runs the same everywhere. 

## Result
The work lead response time fell from 1 day to 2 hours.

## What I learned
Ship a thin end-to-end slice first, then improve the slowest part.
