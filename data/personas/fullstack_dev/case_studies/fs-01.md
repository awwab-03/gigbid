# Subscription SaaS dashboard

**Client:** TeamPulse (synthetic) | **Industry:** SaaS | **Duration:** 6 weeks | **Date:** 2023-04

## The problem
The client was a founder with a prototype but no billing or admin.

## What I did
My approach: a typed React front end, an Express API and plan-based billing with renewal webhooks. The main stack was React, TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL. Under the hood this meant subscription billing and checkout. It also needed utility-first styling. 

## Result
The work launched in 6 weeks; 340 paying teams in the first quarter.

## What I learned
Ship a thin end-to-end slice first, then improve the slowest part.
