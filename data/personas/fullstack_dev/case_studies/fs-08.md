# Supabase migration for a booking app

**Client:** Haven Stays (synthetic) | **Industry:** Hospitality | **Duration:** 4 weeks | **Date:** 2025-02

## The problem
The client was a boutique hotel group outgrowing a no-code backend.

## What I did
My approach: moved data to Postgres with row-level security and rebuilt the booking flow. The main stack was Supabase, Next.js, PostgreSQL. Under the hood this meant strictly typed front-end code. It also needed sign in with Google and GitHub. We looked at Firebase early on but decided not to use it for this engagement.

## Result
The work monthly backend cost cut by 60%; zero downtime cut-over.

## What I learned
Write the data checks before the model or the UI; most surprises came from the data.
