# Restaurant ordering PWA

**Client:** Tandoor House (synthetic) | **Industry:** Food & beverage | **Duration:** 5 weeks | **Date:** 2025-05

## The problem
The client was a restaurant group paying 25% commission to delivery apps.

## What I did
My approach: an installable ordering app with card checkout and a kitchen screen. The main stack was Next.js, Tailwind CSS, Stripe. Under the hood this meant strictly typed front-end code. It also needed an open-source Postgres backend with row-level security. 

## Result
The work direct orders reached 31% of volume within 3 months.

## What I learned
Agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; it saved two rounds of rework.
