# Express API hardening and rate limiting

**Client:** ClearPay (synthetic) | **Industry:** Fintech | **Duration:** 2 weeks | **Date:** 2025-07

## The problem
The client was a payments API hit by credential stuffing.

## What I did
My approach: token rotation, per-client rate limits and audit logging. The main stack was Express, Node.js, Redis. Under the hood this meant sign in with Google and GitHub. 

## Result
The work blocked 99.6% of malicious login attempts with no customer complaints.

## What I learned
Write the data checks before the model or the UI; most surprises came from the data.
